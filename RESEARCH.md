## Heroes

1. **Cairo tanker fire kills 31 — AFRICA / EGYPT — AP.** A fuel
   tanker overturned and caught fire on Cairo's Ring Road at Warraq,
   Giza, on Wednesday, killing at least 31 people and injuring 21,
   charring more than two dozen cars and motorcycles; the toll is
   preliminary (Health Ministry). Tag CONFIRMED (AP + ministry).
   Banner: 31 DEAD IN CAIRO FIRE.

2. **Iran says tankers hit mines in Hormuz — MIDDLE EAST / IRAN —
   Reuters.** Iran's Revolutionary Guards Navy said two oil tankers
   struck naval mines, exploded and caught fire in the Strait of
   Hormuz early on Wednesday while using a route Iran had not
   approved; a third tanker was later reported hit (Fars). The claims
   come only from Iranian statements — no independent confirmation.
   The US Virtual Embassy told US citizens to leave Iran the same
   day. Tag REPORTED (Iranian claim only). Banner: 2 TANKERS HIT
   MINES.

3. **US unveils new tariffs on 14 partners — AMERICAS / TRADE —
   AP, Reuters.** The Trump administration proposed new tariffs under
   Section 301 on 14 trading partners over subsidised factory
   overcapacity: China +20% (45% total on some goods; 40% if the
   truce lapses), Vietnam +20%, Brazil a new 10% aircraft tariff plus
   higher sugar duties, others 10–18%. No duties before a public
   hearing on 25 January 2027. Tag OFFICIAL (USTR). Data-board hero:
   CHINA +20% (45% TOTAL) · BRAZIL 10% AIRCRAFT · 14 PARTNERS ·
   HEARING JAN 25.

## Secondary

4. **Shenzhou 24 launches — ASIA / CHINA — Xinhua.** Shenzhou 24
   lifted off from Jiuquan at 00:18 UTC (08:18 Beijing) on Wednesday
   and docked with Tiangong at 03:56 UTC, carrying commander Tang
   Hongbo, Wu Yi and Pakistani payload specialist Muhammad Zeeshan
   Ali, the first foreign astronaut to visit Tiangong. Tag OFFICIAL
   (CMSA).

5. **Sudan drone strikes kill 19 — AFRICA / SUDAN — Reuters.**
   Rights group Emergency Lawyers said on Wednesday that RSF drone
   strikes killed 19 civilians and wounded 20 in Damazin and
   Roseires, Blue Nile state, on Tuesday night; a hospital source
   gave a lower toll of 14. Tag REPORTED (rights group; strikes Tue
   night, first reported Wed).

6. **Militants kill 30 in Nigeria — AFRICA / NIGERIA — Reuters.**
   Residents said on Wednesday that suspected militants killed at
   least 30 people and burned homes in villages in Niger state on
   Tuesday evening, in retaliation for the killing of Fulani herders
   days earlier; the state governor visited on Wednesday. Tag
   REPORTED (residents; attack Tue evening, first reported Wed).

7. **Israel kills Hamas colonel in Gaza — MIDDLE EAST / GAZA —
   Reuters, Xinhua.** An Israeli strike early on Wednesday killed
   Hamas colonel Mohammad Abuelouf, who headed the militants'
   internal security service in northern Gaza, with his wife and two
   daughters (15 and 13) in the Shati camp, Gaza City; a second
   strike in Karama killed one more. The second senior Hamas security
   official killed in 24 hours (Abu Nahal was killed Tuesday). Tag
   CONFIRMED (Reuters + Xinhua).

8. **Houthis say they hit Ras Tanura — MIDDLE EAST / SAUDI —
   Reuters.** Yemen's Houthis said on Wednesday they attacked Saudi
   Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery, the kingdom's largest (550,000
   barrels/day), and that the strike caused fires; NASA satellite
   imagery showed thick black smoke over ~600 sq km. The Saudi-led
   coalition said air defences destroyed four drones at Ras Tanura
   but did not confirm the refinery was hit. Tag REPORTED (Houthi
   claim; hit unconfirmed).

9. **Australia passes tech duty of care — OCEANIA / AUSTRALIA —
   Reuters, AP.** Australia's parliament passed the Digital Duty of
   Care Act on Wednesday (Senate 36–23, House 97–43): platforms must
   prevent foreseeable online harms or face fines of up to 5% of
   global revenue or A$109m; users gain a right to sue. Takes effect
   in a year. Tag OFFICIAL.

10. **Solomons sign Australia pact — OCEANIA / PACIFIC — AP.**
    Solomon Islands PM Matthew Wale and Australia's Anthony Albanese
    signed a treaty in Canberra on Wednesday making Australia the
    Solomons' "primary security partner", with no foreign military or
    security base allowed on Solomons soil; Australia gives A$740m
    over 10 years. Tag OFFICIAL.

11. **Wall Street whipsaws; GDP 4.3% — AMERICAS / MARKETS —
    Reuters.** The Dow erased a 1,100-point intraday plunge to close
    +0.33% at 50,182.95 on Wednesday; the S&P 500 fell 0.25% to
    7,182.78 and the Nasdaq 0.62% to 23,585.80 after falling as much
    as 4.6%. Q3 GDP was revised up to 4.3%; October PCE inflation
    3.8% year on year, core 3.3%. Tag OFFICIAL (market closes + BEA).

12. **FCC clears 1m SpaceX satellites — AMERICAS / TECH —
    Reuters.** The FCC authorised SpaceX on Wednesday to deploy up to
    1 million satellites for its "Starmind" orbital data-centre
    constellation, a first; deployment must start by November 2033,
    10% by 2035, 50% by 2038. Only ~17,000 satellites are in orbit
    today. Tag OFFICIAL (FCC).

13. **Iowa blast succeeds — AMERICAS / USA — AP.** The US Army
    detonated about 9,500 lb (~5 tons) of degraded lead azide — 68
    barrels — at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant, Middletown, at 09:00
    CST (15:00 UTC) on Wednesday; officials called it a success, with
    no lead found in early EPA air checks. Tag OFFICIAL (Army).
