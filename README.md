## Verification notes


- Every story was verified as having happened, or first been reported,
  on Wednesday 25 November 2026, from a named major outlet. Full
  evidence per story is in `RESEARCH.md`.

- Every illustrated story carries a verification tag — CONFIRMED (two
  or more outlets), OFFICIAL (on the record), REPORTED (one outlet) —
  explained in the footer legend. Disputed figures are shown as claims,
  never as fact. The footer reads: "Claims are attributed, not stated
  as fact."

- Several near-misses were caught in research and excluded: the
  Sudan and Nigeria attacks happened on Tuesday evening, but were
  first reported on Wednesday, so they belong to 25 Nov under the
  competition's own rule; the Mississippi plane crash, Martha Ford's
  death, the Huawei verdict and the China official death sentence were
  all first reported on Tuesday or Monday, even though outlets carried
  updates on Wednesday; Tuesday's Odesa and Kyiv strikes are excluded
  — only Wednesday's fresh attacks are used; the Riyadh tower strike
  (11 hurt) was Tuesday, Wednesday's Saudi story is the Ras Tanura
  claim instead.

- `draw.py` prints no OVERFLOW warnings; counts reconcile
  (3 + 10 + 22 = 35; regions sum 35; 15 fields). `entry.png` is
  1024×1024.
